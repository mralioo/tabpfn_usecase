How this works

Hourly flow per station is compared with its normal: the median of log(1+flow) for that station, day type (weekday / Saturday / Sunday) and hour over the training weeks. The residual divided by that station-hour's robust spread (MAD) is the anomaly score z; surge = z ≥ 2. Models forecast z and the surge probability from information known a day ahead: event calendar (venue mapped to the nearest station), planned closures, weather forecast and station descriptors. An alarm is a station-hour in a model's top 2% of surge scores — the operator's attention budget.

Windows: Alstom hold-out = the provided *_rest files (Sep 22–30, InnoTrans week); Backtest = Sep 1–21 trained on data before Sep 1. Data is Alstom-simulated; many |z| ≥ 2 hours are noise with no driver, which caps precision for every model. Engine: tabpfn_lab/anomaly.py, notebook notebooks/02_anomaly_early_warning.py.
