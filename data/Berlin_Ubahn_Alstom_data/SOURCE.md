# Data provenance — Berlin U-Bahn

See [`../SOURCE.md`](../SOURCE.md) for the overview of all three datasets this repo uses. This
file covers the Berlin-specific details.

The CSV files under `training dataset/` and `testing dataset/` are **not collected by this
project**. They were generated by **Alstom's data team** for the **InnoTrans 2026 hackathon**
(Berlin U-Bahn operations scenario): real station/line/weather geometry combined with
simulated passenger flow, closures and energy-consumption series. Full column-by-column
schema: [`dataset_schema.md`](dataset_schema.md) (copied from the organiser's brief).

They are reused here, unmodified, for the **TabPFN-3.5 hackathon** (platform.priorlabs.ai) under
that hackathon's rule 3.2 ("the linked repository must contain ... your input data or make it
available at a public URL"). This repo makes no ownership claim over the data itself — only over
the code in `tabpfn_lab/`, `mcp_server/`, `agent/`, `webapp/`, `notebooks/` and `scripts/`.

## Splits

| Folder | Files | Covers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `training dataset/` | stations, lines, connections, flows, weather, events, closures, energy | 2026-06-10 → 2026-09-21 (`*_pre_innotrans.csv`) |
| `testing dataset/` | flows, weather, events, closures, energy (reuses the training folder's station/line/connection files) | 2026-09-22 → 2026-10-01 (`*_rest.csv`) |

The other two datasets (Deutsche Bahn, Finnish railway) are unrelated to this one — see
[`../SOURCE.md`](../SOURCE.md) for their provenance and the project's lineage from NextMove.
